## Logo Usage

- Use the mixed red/black logo as the default on off-white or white backgrounds.
- Use the full red logo when the design needs a stronger campaign/editorial tone on light backgrounds.
- Use the full black logo for restrained monochrome sections.
- Use the white logo treatment on black, red, or image-based backgrounds.
- Keep generous spacing around the logo. Do not place it inside a heavy card or decorative container.

## Visual Direction For The Website

Scaleshift is a reels editing portfolio brand. The website should feel editorial, sharp, motion-led, and confident. Use the red as a precise accent and the off-white/black pair as the main structure. Avoid generic agency gradients, oversized decorative cards, and soft pastel layouts that weaken the logo.

Recommended page rhythm:

1. Hero with strong logo presence, short positioning line, and reel/video-led visual.
2. Portfolio reel grid or vertical video showcase.
3. Editing services and deliverables.
4. Process section: raw footage to final reel.
5. Proof/case-study section.
6. Contact/booking form.

## Brief For `/impeccable`

Design a polished portfolio website for Scaleshift, a company specialized in editing short-form reels. Use the attached brand identity as the source of truth. The official palette is OffWhite `#F2EBEB`, Bright Red `#ED0F24`, Black `#000000`, and White `#FFFFFF`. Use the mixed red/black logo as the primary logo on light backgrounds, red or black variants only when the section treatment calls for it, and white logo treatment on dark/image backgrounds.

The site should feel editorial, sharp, premium, motion-led, and built around vertical video. Create a complete multi-section landing/portfolio page, not just a hero. Include hero, reel showcase, services, process, case studies/proof, and contact sections. Keep UI text code-native and practical for a Next.js implementation. Avoid invented colors, generic SaaS gradients, excessive rounded cards, and decorative filler.
